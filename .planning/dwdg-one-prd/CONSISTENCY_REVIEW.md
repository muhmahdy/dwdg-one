## Material engineering repairs

- Process definitions precede journey verification; the root/quality cycle was removed.
- Optional email/WhatsApp, native invoice tracking, timesheets, paid services and binary uploads do not become mandatory launch edges.
- Default export/recovery excludes external-provider document bytes. A link manifest is not a backup of those files.
- Free-subdomain deployment does not require domain purchase or paid Pro. Budget remains Rp35,000/month target, Rp50,000 maximum including adopted fees/amortization.
- Candidate deployment precedes pilot entry; observed pilot precedes the broader launch gate.
- Real-data restoration needs a restricted isolated destination, separate from member training sandbox.
- Commands, current-grant authorization/revocation, transactional data/audit, consistent exports and verified auth remapping have explicit contracts.
