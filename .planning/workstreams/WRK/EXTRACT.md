### Teams define their own routines and operations
`work_routines` · confirmed · P0 · parent `workflows` · owner: Product + engineering owners

Owner decision D31: divisions and branches create their own recurring operations (for example MarcomIT’s weekly or fortnightly content posting) without asking a developer. A routine definition has a name, owning unit, cadence (weekly, every two weeks, monthly or a custom interval, in Asia/Jakarta), start date, an optional checklist of steps, the default owner or a rotation among named members, and a due offset. It generates separate occurrences; each occurrence has its own canonical tasks, owner, due date and completion, and finishing one never finishes another (work_type_model). Leaders can pause, skip or reschedule a single occurrence, or change the definition for future occurrences only; past occurrences keep their history. Co-Director and above create and edit unit routines (same threshold as projects); any member can make their own repeating personal task. Routine tasks show the routine as their context chip (D15). Owner decision D34 (6 Oct 2026): routines live in a top-level Operations area beside Projects, because operations differ from bounded projects; Projects keep bounded work only. The user-facing word for an occurrence is a run (putaran in Indonesian). A single run can be moved to another day, and a moved run keeps its new date when the definition later changes. Runs appear in the owner’s My Work up to three weeks ahead. Open for the owner: whether Programs and Requests also live under Operations. Owner decision D35 (confirmed 6 Oct 2026): sign-off is required for a task someone else gave you (accepted offers and assignments), for every run of a division routine (the routine leader can turn this off), and for any task a leader marks as needing it; not for your own tasks or personal repeats. The signer is, in order: a person chosen for that task, the routine’s chosen signer, the person who gave the task, the project PM, the project lead, then the division’s leaders by rank; never the owner or a co-assignee. When the owner marks it done it moves to In review with the signer as reviewer and the signer is notified; only the signer, a leader above the owner, the giver, or the project lead or PM can record Completed (stored as signed by and signed at), and they can return it with a reason. The owner cannot pick their own reviewer for these tasks. Members cannot skip or move a routine run themselves; they send a request with a reason and a leader approves or declines it (recorded and notified). D47: an operation may belong to one program.

**Acceptance:**
1. A Co-Director creates a fortnightly routine; 3 generated occurrences have 3 independent IDs, owners and completion states.
2. Editing the definition changes 0 past occurrences; skipping one occurrence leaves the next unchanged.
3. A Member cannot create a unit routine (neutral denial) but can create a repeating personal task.

**Prerequisites:** `work_type_model`, `work_task_fields`, `access_project_creation_cd`, `org_timezone_calendar` (ARC)
