### Minimal HR and recruitment privacy
`security_hr` · proposed · P0 · parent `security` · owner: Product owner + technical maintainer (named people TBD)

General membership directory shows necessary organizational identity. Applications, reviewer notes and personal contact detail are restricted to designated reviewers and the applicant where applicable. Avoid medical information, government ID images and free-text sensitive profiling. Stage changes are explicit decisions with a responsible reviewer; dashboards report bounded counts without identifying rejected applicants.

**Acceptance:**
Ordinary members receive 0 private candidate/reviewer/contact fields through detail/search/count/Changes/export APIs. Valid reviewer and permitted applicant cases are recorded separately; recruitment collection cannot pass launch before purpose/retention/authority is approved.

**Prerequisites:** `security_permissions` (AGT), `entity_recruitment`, `data_retention` (POL)

### One HR monitoring-and-grading cycle every 14 days
`hr_fortnightly_cycle` · confirmed · P0 · parent `division_hr` · owner: Proposed policy owner + engineering

Confirmed every two weeks, not twice weekly/monthly. Proposed consecutive anchored windows; eligible roster/unit snapshot/reviewers/rubric; independent submission/review/correction deadlines. New-joiner/transfer/leave/exempt policy open.

**Acceptance:**
1. Three anchored periods each 14 calendar days, zero gaps/overlap.
2. Closing one does not complete later cycles or erase support actions.
3. Eligible/submitted/reviewed/incomplete/exempt counts reconcile.

**Prerequisites:** `org_timezone_calendar` (ARC), `hr_validation`

### Adopt rubric, evidence, reviewer and correction rules
`hr_scoring_policy` · open · P0 · parent `division_hr` · owner: Proposed policy owner + engineering

Grades requested. OPEN categories/maximums/weights/scale/anchors/audience. Proposed member evidence -> reviewer draft -> HR completeness/exceptions -> finalized version -> own feedback/correction. Missing evidence Incomplete, not automatic zero; record raw values/weight/source/rationale/reviewer/version. No task-count-only ranking.

**Acceptance:**
1. One rubric with recalculable numeric examples adopted.
2. Missing criterion is incomplete per rule; zero invented scores.
3. Correction retains old/new/actor/reason and member own access.

**Prerequisites:** `hr_fortnightly_cycle`, `access_sensitive_data`

### Member report, reviewed feedback and development handoff
