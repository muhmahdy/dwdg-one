Map of the app

Two groups: Me (always personal) and Workspace (switched between the six divisions). Everything else is global.

Sign in (Google) ─► Waiting for approval ─► My Work ME My Work (landing) · Schedule WORKSPACE [switcher: 6 divisions · "All of DWDG" for President/Admin] Projects ─► Project page: Overview · Work · Resources Resources (folders · notes · links · pinned) GLOBAL People · Settings (Admin: Members & approvals, Export) · ⌘K search · + New (Task · Meeting · Note · Link · Project*) * Directors and Admin only

Key flows

F2 · Capture a task in 5 seconds

  1. Press N or + New › Task
  2. Type “Draft recruitment post copy Fri @Rani”
  3. Date, person and project/division turn into chips
  4. ⏎ saves; Undo toast

F8 · Schedule a meeting

  1. Press M or + New › Meeting
  2. Type “Sync with Rani and Dimas tomorrow 45 min”
  3. One availability row per person appears
  4. Pick a suggested time or drag the slot
  5. Send invitations ⏎

F9 · Answer an invitation

  1. It waits in My Work › Needs your response
  2. Accept · Decline · Suggest a time, inline
  3. Accepted meetings join Today and Schedule

F7 · Mark unavailable

  1. Schedule › Unavailable
  2. Drag on the week, or type “Classes Mon Wed 08–11 weekly”
  3. Preview blocks, choose who sees the note
  4. Save

F4 · Create a project (Directors)

  1. Projects › + New project
  2. Name, goal, lead, stage; dates may stay unknown
  3. Lands on the Overview tab

F6 · Add a resource

  1. + Link: paste URL, title, folder
  2. + Note: write, saved as revisions
  3. “Make a task from this” opens F2 prefilled

Replaces a dashboard of widgets. Three questions in order: what needs my answer, what is today, what is coming. Rows read like sentences.
