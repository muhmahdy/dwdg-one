Tokens, components and the division patterns for dwdg’ONE. Source: .planning/design/system/

Colour

One neutral family, one accent (the official green), and semantic colours only where they carry meaning. Each swatch shows light · dark. Text colours meet 4.5:1 on their surfaces in both themes; marks and icons meet 3:1.

Space, corners and depth

A 4px spacing scale. Three corner sizes: sheets 12, controls 8, chips 6; avatars and toggles are round. Depth comes from tone and a hairline ring, never from side stripes.

Icons and avatars

Interface icons: one 1.8px stroke set from the existing app. Identity icons: the role and division set. People are circular photos; a monogram is used only when there is no photo.

Buttons

One primary per view. Secondary has a hairline ring; ghost has none. Press scales to 98%.

Label above, help or error below. Placeholders never replace labels. Required is marked; drafts are kept on failure.
